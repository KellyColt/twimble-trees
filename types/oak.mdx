title: Oak
description: boring wood for boring people
---

<Note>
  Before beginning, make sure the farm is fully off!
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="bonemeals you">
    Begin by enabling [The Bonemealer (TM)](/sup/bonemelee), setting it's Output to Main Farm and enabling the Output Engine. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="configures you">
    Returning to the General Tree Farm, climb up and look straight to find the Redstone Dust indicated by a sign. It should be set to a Dot, not a Cross, for all except Jungle Tree Farming. If it is a Cross, right click (interact) with it to change the shape. 

    Hop over the water to your right, and climb the stairs up onto an upper platform, then look to your left to find a scaffolding tower. Climb this tower up until its first bend, walk forwards on it to find a lever. Double check that this Lever is flicked to the left, as a rightways lever is Jungle Mode. Return down the way you came. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="filters you">
    Back at the Area below with the Main Switch, look below the carpet there to find the Item Filter Hoppers. A guide on configuring them can be found here: [Item Filters](/item-filters)

    The General Tree Farm does not automatically recycle any of its drops back into Bonemeal, so there is no danger of losing items if not configured; however, to make your life easier, you can put three Item Filters in place that will automatically collect the filtered item into the double chest below it. 

    Suggested Item Filters are: 

    1. Wood Logs
    2. Saplings 
    3. Misc (Sticks, Apples) 
  </Step>
  <Step title="farms you">
    Prepare your Inventory and begin farming as described in [Basic Operation](/basics). You will be fed Bonemeal by the Bonemeal Dispenser, and replenish saplings from broken leaf blocks. 
  </Step>
</Steps>
