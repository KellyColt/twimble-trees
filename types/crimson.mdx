description: Wood for the Blood God

Huge Fungi have their own dedicated farm. This farm is completely independent from all other circuits, meaning it does not require the Bonemealer to be configured. This is because it replenishes its own bonemeal consumption! Neat! 

<Info>
  If you mysteriously lose items while using the Farm, or the Bonemeal Circuit seems to not be working, let me know! The Bonemeal Circuit swallows up any items not filtered out before its input, but any non-compostable items simply wind up clogging up its hoppers. I can retrieve them for you! 
</Info>

<Note>
  Before beginning, make sure the farm is fully off!
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="configures you">
    The Huge Fungi Farm needs to be configured before starting any of its circuits! This Farm's configuration is a little more involved, but since it only has two settings (Crimson and Warped), you have good odds of it already being set for you. 

    Firstly, you will need to replace the Nylium in the center of the collection area with the one corresponding to your goal Wood. Remember, Nylium can only be farmed with a Pickaxe that has the Silk Touch enchantment! In a pinch, you can remove the old Nylium without retaining it, however, it is polite to make sure there is a block or two of each type of Nylium remaining in the farm's storage. 

    <Warning>
      To make sure you do not accidentally clog the bonemeal circuit, enter the Farm and flip down the Trapdoor to the immediate right to disable bonemeal cycling! Otherwise, your Nylium and any temporary blocks might wind up disappearing down that way! 
    </Warning>

    <Frame>
      ![2026 10 04 14 47 31](/images/2026-10-04_14.47.31.png "2026 10 04 14 47 31")
    </Frame>

    As you can see in the image, there are potentially two Nylium Blocks; the lower one is the one to replace. The other one should be changed into Netherrack, either by placing a block on top of it temporarily or by breaking and replacing it. 

    Replacing this Nylium can be tricky. It is recommended to tower up with spare blocks until level with it or one block below, then flip down the trapdoor. Now you can crouch, break the current Nylium, then place the new one using the trapdoor as your surface (still crouching). 

    <Tip>
      Since all the water in the collection basin runs from the corners, you are in no danger of breaking this system by placing blocks inside it. However, make sure you remove your temporary blocks after you are done! 
    </Tip>

    The second block that needs to be replaced is the Nylium in the Fungi Sapling Replenisher. Enter the Farm, then turn right and hop up over the Main Switch, then up again to the left. Cross the scaffolding and go on top of the bonemeal chests to find a cobblestone with a ladder on its other side. This is your access to the Replenisher. 

    In the center of the platform, there will be a single block of Nylium that needs to be replaced if it does not match your intended Wood Type. Crouching next to it as you break it is the easiest way to make sure you pick it back up.

    <Frame>
      ![2026 10 04 15 22 50](/images/2026-10-04_15.22.50.png "2026 10 04 15 22 50")
    </Frame>

    Now the scary part is done! All that is left is ensuring the Bonemeal Cycle trapdoor to the right from the entrance is flipped up, and configuring the [Item Filters](/item-filters)! 

    The farm can only replenish its own Bonemeal by recycling Wart Blocks and other drops from the farm operation. To make sure the bonemeal cycle isn't clogged by logs and ensure you actually get those logs for your projects, you will need to at minimum configure one item filter to pick up those logs. Another item filter for extra Fungi saplings is also a good idea! 
  </Step>
  <Step title="turns you on ;)">
    Turning on the Huge Fungi Farm is largely identical to other farms. The only additional step is enabling the Sapling Replenisher, the lever for which can be found diagonally above of the entrance ladders, below two trapdoors. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="farms you">
    From here, farming is identical to [Basic Operation](/basics)! Happy Fungi Farming! 
  </Step>
</Steps>
