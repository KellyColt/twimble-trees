Item Filters are found in many places around the tw.Tree Farms' configurations. 

<Warning>
  Make sure you only change Filters when explicitly told to by this wiki! You can easily break a farm by messing with filters that aren't meant for configuration! 
</Warning>

</Frame>

An Item Filter's main Interface is its topmost Hopper. In it, you should see one stack of <Tooltip tip="This number is based on total stack size, so it can differ for items which stack to 16. Items which do not stack cannot be filtered!">41 Items</Tooltip>, plush 4 single items in the other 4 slots. You only ever need to care about that very first stack of 41! 

To reconfigure the Filter, start by making a stack of **at most 41** of your new filtered item. If you put more, you can break the Filter! Less is fine, though know that the filter will hold onto any new items it filters until it hits that 41 stack size. 

Once you have your new stack, go ahead and replace the current stack with it; you can take the old one out first, nothing bad will happen, just don't touch the other four items. 

<Danger>
  In the rare case that your activities should have potential of producing the items in the other slots, you need to replace them with other single items that cannot be produced! 
</Danger>
